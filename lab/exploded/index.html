Parts list

Each part is a block of rows in one or more sheets. Cells, formulas, catalog inputs and outputs are counted from the converted workbook. Formula links count every cell-to-cell precedent crossing into or out of the part, so a single formula that reads a 193-month row adds 193 links.

Structure is read from the converted workbook: 54,505 cells and 1,656,807 formula links, grouped into parts by sheet rows. Every pipe is a bundle of real cell-to-cell links.
Numbers come from a grid of 1,600 engine runs, one per throttle combination. The page looks runs up; it never interpolates or computes a figure. Part speeds are scaled from those engine outputs for animation only.
