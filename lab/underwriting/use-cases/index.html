Budgeting

Budget season. Twelve communities, one workbook each. The CFO asks: if wages rise 4% instead of 3%, which communities miss the covenant?

You can expect

  • Every “what if” answered across all twelve, while you're still in the meeting.
  • No more copying the workbook to test a case and losing track of which copy is right.
  • Site teams ask their own questions; nobody edits the master.
