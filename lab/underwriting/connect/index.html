Connect your AI

Ask the model from your own AI assistant.

Excel’s answers, without Excel, for any AI. Paste one paragraph into Meta Muse, Claude or ChatGPT. Your assistant connects to the underwriting model, unzipped from its Excel workbook into the small program someone actually wrote, and asks it directly. Every number still comes from the model, not from the AI. No signup.
